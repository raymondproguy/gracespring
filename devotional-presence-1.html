Awakened to His Presence, Episode 1

Awake to His presence

GraceSpring Family ChurchChurch admin, 4 days ago

Do you only think of God on Sundays, or do you carry the awareness of Him through the day?

“Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee from thy presence?”

Psalm 139:7

God does not arrive when we pray. He is already there. Prayer is us becoming aware.

Practise noticing Him: in the morning, in traffic, at your desk, in the quiet before sleep.

Reality check

Where was I least aware of God yesterday?

Lord, open my eyes to You in every ordinary moment. In Jesus' name, Amen.
