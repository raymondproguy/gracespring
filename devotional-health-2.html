Supernatural Health, Episode 2

Faith is your evidence

GraceSpring Family ChurchChurch admin, today

Your body is real. But sickness is not the only reality you should be conscious of.

What place does God's Word occupy in your daily thinking about your health?

Proverbs 4:20–22 urges us to attend to God's words and keep them in our hearts. That Scripture describes them as “life unto those that find them, and health to all their flesh.”

God's Word must be more than something we read when trouble comes. It should shape our faith, strengthen our hope and guide how we live.

People who do sports do not wait till there are competitions to train. They stay fit.

Don't let the fear of sickness occupy the space meant for the truth of God's Word.

Feed your faith. Walk in wisdom. Care for your body. Stay attentive to the Holy Spirit.

And when you need medical care, receive it responsibly without abandoning your trust in God.

Reality check

What am I allowing to shape my expectations about my health? Is it fear alone, or a faith continually nourished by God's Word?

Faith is my evidence.
